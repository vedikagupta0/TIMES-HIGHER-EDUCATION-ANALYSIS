# 🎓 Times Higher Education World University Rankings Analysis

A comprehensive Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) of the Times Higher Education (THE) World University Rankings dataset, aimed at understanding the factors that drive institutional excellence, comparing national higher education ecosystems, and identifying actionable strategies for universities and policymakers.

## Project Overview

Universities are central to innovation, research, economic development, and global talent creation. This project analyzes 2,191 universities across more than 90 countries using the Times Higher Education World University Rankings dataset.

The analysis investigates:

- Which countries dominate global rankings
- What differentiates top-performing universities
- How teaching, research, citations, and industry engagement interact
- The effect of student-to-staff ratios on performance
- The role of internationalization in institutional success
- Country-level strengths, weaknesses, and strategic trade-offs

The objective is not only to describe ranking outcomes but also to uncover patterns that can support better decision-making in higher education.

---

## Dataset

**Source:** Times Higher Education (THE) World University Rankings

### Coverage

- 2,191 universities
- 90+ countries
- Global ranking data

### Core Metrics

The ranking methodology evaluates universities across five major dimensions:

| Metric | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Teaching | Learning environment and teaching quality |
| Research | Research volume, reputation, and productivity |
| Citations | Research influence and impact |
| Industry Income | Knowledge transfer and industry collaboration |
| International Outlook | International students, faculty, and collaborations |

Additional institutional characteristics include:

- Student population
- Student-to-staff ratio
- International student percentage
- Gender distribution
- Country information
